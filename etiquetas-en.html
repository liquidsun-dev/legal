Summary

Everything you save in Etiquetas stays only on your phone. There is no user account and no server of our own: we do not receive, see or store your orders, your buyers' details, your screenshots, your videos or your photos.

Files only leave the phone when you save them to the gallery or send them through Android's Share menu.

The app is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob and by an optional subscription (Premium) paid through Google Play. With Premium, for the timestamp only a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the certificate leaves the phone, never the file or the order details.

The app does not use your location, has no analytics of its own and sends no usage reports.

Sharing and gallery

Etiquetas doesn't upload anything on its own. When you save a video or photo to the gallery, it stays in your phone's Movies/ and Pictures/ folders. When you tap Share (a video, the message for the buyer, the sealed package, the backup…) or choose Telegram, the app hands the file or text to Android's Share menu. You choose the app (WhatsApp, Telegram, email, Drive…) and the recipient; from then on that app's policy applies.

Authenticity seal (Premium)

With Premium, each video or photo can carry a signed certificate. The signature uses a key that is created and stays in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore); we don't have it.

For the timestamp, the app computes the SHA-256 fingerprint of the certificate (a 32-byte string) and sends it to a timestamping authority (FreeTSA, freetsa.org, and DigiCert, timestamp.digicert.com) and to the public OpenTimestamps calendar servers. The fingerprint cannot be used to rebuild the video, the photo or any order detail: it only serves to prove later that the certificate already existed at that time. As with any internet connection, those services receive your IP address.

The seal proves that the file existed at that time at the latest and has not been modified. It is not a qualified eIDAS timestamp and we do not guarantee that a court or a selling platform will accept it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you don't have Premium, the app shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.): a banner at the bottom of some screens and, only occasionally, a full-screen ad after you print a sheet. There are no ads while you record.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows Google's official form (User Messaging Platform) so you can decide whether you accept personalized ads. In US states with privacy laws, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy. If you don't consent, ads will not be personalized.

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may process the advertising ID, the IP address and technical data about the device and ad usage. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads). Google processes this under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Google's page on advertising.

Nothing you save in Etiquetas (orders, buyers, screenshots, videos) is sent to Google or used to choose the ads.

Subscription (Premium)

Premium is a quarterly or yearly subscription bought through the Google Play billing system, with a 7-day free trial. We neither see nor store your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has Premium active. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled on Google Play.

Android permissions

  • Camera: to scan codes (including the QR codes on storage boxes) and record packing videos and photos. It is only used when you open those screens.
  • Microphone: for the sound of the videos you record.
  • Storage (Android 9 or earlier only): to save videos and photos to the gallery.
  • Clipboard: to paste label screenshots and pick up the Telegram message link when you copy it.
  • Internet: for ads, the subscription and the timestamp.

Android shows its own indicator in the status bar while the camera or microphone is in use; the app cannot hide it.

Backup

In Settings › Backup you can create a file with your data and save it or send it with Share wherever you like; the app doesn't send it anywhere on its own. If Android's automatic backup is on, the system may include the app's data in your Google account backup; that backup is managed by Google and can be turned off in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete closed orders or use Settings › Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Videos and photos you saved to the gallery stay there until you delete them yourself. What Google AdMob receives is governed by its own policy.

Independent app

Etiquetas is not affiliated with any courier, shipping office or selling platform, and does not connect to their systems: it only reads what appears in the screenshots you give it.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (and similar laws in other countries) you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability.

In this app you exercise most of these rights yourself, directly: the data is on your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it completely. We keep no copy that we could return or delete on your behalf.

For any question about this processing, write to liquidsun.dev@gmail.com. If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the authority of your country.
