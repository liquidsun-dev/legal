Summary in three sentences

There is no user account and no server of our own. Your saved places, your routes and your walk history stay on your device.

The Android app is funded by ads from Google AdMob, and in the European Union you are asked first. There is no analytics of any other kind.

You can use the app without giving it access to GPS: just type the name of a city or paste some coordinates.

Advertising

The Android app is free and paid for by ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, the AdMob SDK accesses the device’s advertising ID and data about how the app is used.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the app you are asked whether you accept personalized ads. You can change that choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, under Google › Ads. More information on Google’s advertising page.

The web version shows no ads.

Third-party services the app queries

To show the weather and maps, data has to be requested over the internet. The service queried receives your IP address and the coordinates of the area you are looking at:

  • MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute) — weather forecast. (MET Norway terms)
  • Photon (komoot), with OpenStreetMap data — searching places by name. (komoot policy)
  • OpenStreetMap — the map images and walking and cycling routes. (OSM policy)

None of these services receives an identifier of yours: there is no account to link it to.

Android permissions

  • Location — optional, to use your position and record walks.
  • Internet — for the weather, the map, routes and ads.

Retention

Everything saved lives in the storage of the app or of your browser. Clearing its data, or uninstalling it, removes it completely. What Google AdMob receives is governed by its own policy.

Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation gives you the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing and ask for its portability. Depending on where you live, local laws (for example in some US states) may give you similar rights.

In this app you exercise most of those rights yourself, directly and without intermediaries: the data is on your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it completely. We keep no copy that we could return to you or delete on your behalf. We do not sell your personal data.

For any question about this processing you can write to liquidsun.dev@gmail.com. If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the authority of your country.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will reflect it.
