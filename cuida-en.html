Privacy policy

Cuida — com.liquidsun.cuida

Organize your parents' care as a family: medication, appointments, tasks, shifts, readings and emergencies.

Summary

Everything you enter in Cuida is stored only on your phone. There is no user account and no server of our own: we do not receive, see or store the medication, the appointments, the readings or any other data about the person you care for.

If you share data with your family (the “family package”, the summary or the report), you do it yourself through Android's Share menu, and it only reaches whoever you choose.

The app is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob and by an optional subscription (Premium) paid through Google Play. Your health data is never used for ads or sent to Google.

Much of this is health data about another person (the one you care for). As it stays on your device and you use it within the family, we never process it. We recommend getting their agreement where possible and protecting your phone with a screen lock.

Sharing with the family

Cuida doesn't sync anything on its own. When you tap Send family package, Send summary, Doctor's report, Back up now or Share a document, the app prepares a file or a text and hands it to Android's Share menu. You choose the app (WhatsApp, email, Drive…) and the recipient; from then on that app's policy applies. When you open a package you received, its data is merged with yours on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you don't have Premium, the app shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.): a banner at the bottom, full-screen ads at some natural breaks (for example after saving something or when returning to the app after a while), with a frequency limit, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an extra. The emergency screen has no ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, before requesting any ad the app shows Google's official form (User Messaging Platform) so you can decide whether you accept personalized ads. In US states with privacy laws, the same form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in More › Ad privacy. If you don't consent, ads will not be personalized.

To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may process the advertising ID, the IP address and technical data about the device and ad usage. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads). Google processes this under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Google's page on advertising.

Nothing you enter in Cuida is sent to Google or used to choose the ads.

Purchases (Cuida Premium)

Premium is bought through the Google Play billing system. We neither see nor store your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has Premium active. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled on Google Play.

Reminders

Medication and appointment reminders are scheduled by the phone itself, without any server. Their text (medication and person's name) may be visible on the lock screen; you can hide it in Android's notification settings.

Android permissions

  • Internet: only for ads and purchases.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and start on boot: so medication reminders ring on time, also after restarting the phone.
  • Google Play Billing: to buy Premium.
  • Advertising ID: used by the AdMob SDK.

Document photos are chosen with the Android picker: the app does not ask for permission to read your gallery or use the camera on its own.

Backup

In More › Settings and backups you can create a file with your data (without the photos) and save it wherever you like; the app doesn't send it anywhere on its own. If Android's automatic backup is on, the system may include the app's data in your Google account backup; that backup is managed by Google and can be turned off in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything in More › Settings and backups › Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app. What Google AdMob receives is governed by its own policy.

Not a medical device

Cuida is an organizing tool. It gives no diagnoses or medical advice, does not interpret readings and does not replace a doctor or pharmacist. Reminders may be delayed if the phone's battery saver blocks them.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (and similar laws in other countries) you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability.

In this app you exercise most of these rights yourself, directly: the data is on your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it completely. We keep no copy that we could return or delete on your behalf.

For any question about this processing, write to liquidsun.dev@gmail.com. If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the authority of your country.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 in the United States) and does not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

Changes to this policy
